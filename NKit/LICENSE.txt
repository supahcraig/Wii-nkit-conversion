and GameTDB community databases, and per-title recovery files) - they're

pipeline documented in ../NKit-to-WBFS-HOWTO.md if you already know your
game's ID. Get a full copy (with Dats/) from
https://github.com/emukidid/swiss-gc/releases if you want auto-identification.
